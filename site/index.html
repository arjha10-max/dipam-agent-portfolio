D Data Insight foundation

The sample research positions Vividance between plain hydration, sports drinks, and high-stimulation energy drinks. The leading synthetic insight is:

When people want a functional beverage for demanding routines, they want a clear benefit without the baggage of an energy-drink occasion — so the brand should lead with calm, everyday hydration rather than performance intensity.

Decision recorded: prioritize occasion clarity and credibility over aggressive functional claims.

04 · Governance

The agent has boundaries.

It can

Extract briefs, synthesize approved inputs, draft plans, reconcile documents, maintain trackers, and recommend actions.

It cannot

Approve itself, sign an MPA or IO, launch campaigns, move raw PII, make unsupported claims, or invent performance data.

Humans own

Strategy, client commitments, legal and privacy decisions, platform execution, pre-flight QA, and optimization approval.

05 · Portability

Same operating model. Different category logic.

Vividance and DermaNova use the same gates and templates, but the category evidence, audience motivations, claims guardrails, retail dependencies, and measurement windows change.

06 · Value model

A deliberately honest ROI case.

The expected illustrative case shows $100,920 in margin-adjusted value against a $45,000 pilot cost — a 124.3% illustrative ROI. The conservative case is close to break-even. Every input is an assumption until measured in a pilot.
