## 2. Business context

DermaNova is preparing to launch **Barrier Reset Serum**, a fictional daily facial serum positioned for people whose skin feels tight, dry, or stressed by weather, travel, over-exfoliation, or frequent product switching.

The product concept uses ceramides, glycerin, and niacinamide. The brand wants a credible, education-led position without medical claims or fear-based messaging.

The launch challenge is to win consideration in a crowded skincare market while building trust among shoppers who compare ingredients, reviews, dermatologist cues, and price across retail channels.

## 3. Objectives

### Business objective

Build a repeatable new-brand launch model by generating trial, replenishment, and retailer-ready learning in Canada and the United States.

### Marketing objective

Position DermaNova as a simple, reassuring barrier-care step for people who want comfortable, resilient-looking skin without a complicated routine.

### Media objective

Balance broad education with high-intent retail conversion, then shift investment toward the messages, audiences, and retail paths that produce qualified purchases and repeat behavior.

### Measurement objective

Connect creative education, retail intent, conversion, and replenishment while respecting consent and avoiding sensitive health or skin-condition targeting.

## 8. Audience hypotheses

### Current buyers

Adults aged 25–44 who currently buy facial serums, moisturizers, dermocosmetic skincare, or ingredient-led beauty products.

### Growth audiences

1. Routine simplifiers who want one dependable barrier-care product instead of a multi-step regimen.
2. Ingredient researchers who compare product labels, creator education, reviews, and retailer content before purchase.

### Audiences to exclude

- Minors
- Audiences built from diagnosed or inferred medical skin conditions
- Users who have withdrawn consent or are not eligible for activation in a given market

### Audience data available

Synthetic first-party email list, ecommerce analytics, retailer clean-room estimates, contextual beauty-content signals, and platform-native interest tools.
