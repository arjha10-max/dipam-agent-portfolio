# Brand Lead Narrative — From Brief to Launch with DIPAM

> Synthetic portfolio narrative. Vividance and DermaNova are fictional brands. All numbers are illustrative planning assumptions, not client results or market claims.

## Slide 1 — A better way to launch

### One brief. One governed workflow. One visible decision trail.

The DIPAM agent helps a brand team move from brief to launch with fewer unclear handoffs. It structures the work, makes assumptions visible, and stops for the right approvals before strategy becomes activation.

The goal is not more process. The goal is better decisions with less rework.

## Slide 2 — What changes for the brand team

### The agent turns ambiguity into explicit decisions.

At Intake, it identifies:

- What the brief asks
- Which KPIs and budgets are clear
- Which product claims need review
- Which data assets are available
- Which questions must be answered
- Who needs to approve the next stage

The brand team sees gaps before they become launch delays.

## Slide 3 — Strategy becomes usable

### Research and audiences are built for activation.

The Data stage creates a focused evidence base: category context, consumer behavior, competitors, price-pack logic, trends, and data readiness.

The Identity stage then creates exactly three profiles by default:

- One CORE audience to defend and upsell
- Two GROWTH audiences to recruit new buyers

Each profile includes motivations, occasions, barriers, message triggers, value, consent approach, activation route, and measurement hypothesis.

## Slide 4 — Creative and media stay connected

### Personalization becomes an integrated approval pack.

The agent connects each audience to:

- A message and proof point
- A creative territory
- A modular variant system
- A platform and vendor role
- A budget allocation
- A KPI target
- A test-and-learn priority

This makes it easier to approve one coherent plan instead of reconciling separate creative and media decks.

## Slide 5 — Activation risk is visible earlier

### MPA, IOs, creative specs, tags, and QA are tracked together.

After simulated MPA sign-off, the agent supports two parallel tracks:

- **Connections/Implementation Planning:** MPA reconciliation, vendor negotiations, IOs, blocking charts, budget, billing, and sign-offs
- **Activation/Ad Operations:** tagging, trafficking, audience loading, frequency, brand safety, QA, go-live, and 72-hour monitoring

A dependency diagram shows what must be complete before launch.

## Slide 6 — Measurement is designed before results arrive

### The agent will not invent performance data.

Measurement starts only after a performance export is supplied. It then creates:

- KPI-tree reporting
- Platform, market, audience, creative, and retailer cuts
- A clearly labelled simplified MMM-lite view
- Incrementality and brand-lift test designs
- Anomaly and escalation rules
- Optimization recommendations with confidence levels

Learning feeds back into the next campaign cycle.

## Slide 7 — Scaling across the portfolio

### Standardize the workflow, not the answer.

Vividance and DermaNova show how the same operating model adapts across categories:

- Different product truths and claims
- Different audience motivations
- Different retail paths
- Different repeat or replenishment windows
- Different media and measurement needs

The gates, templates, state model, and privacy controls remain consistent.
